Understand your Life Status Point history.

A Chrome extension that saves the LSP information already shown in your JAL account, analyzes changes locally, and estimates progress toward Star Grade LSP requirements.

Independent, unofficial tool

JAL LSP Tracker is independently developed. It is not affiliated with, authorized by, endorsed by, or produced by Japan Airlines Co., Ltd. “JAL”, “JMB”, product names, and related marks belong to their respective owners.

What it does

  • Reads the total Life Status Point value and history already visible after the user signs in to JAL.
  • Saves records only in the current Chrome profile using chrome.storage.local.
  • Shows trends, source breakdowns, and estimates for reaching Star Grade LSP requirements.
  • Exports local records as CSV or JSON and lets the user delete them at any time.

The extension has no developer-operated backend, advertising, analytics SDK, or remote executable code.
